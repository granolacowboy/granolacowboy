- 45 non-forks.
- 1,200 / 1,205 forks were created in 2025.

Interpretation: account clutter is overwhelmingly a historical bulk-fork problem. The optimal first pass is deterministic fork/source/star reconciliation, not semantic repository review. The 25 post-fork-push exceptions, the five 2026 forks, and all original repositories should be treated more conservatively.
